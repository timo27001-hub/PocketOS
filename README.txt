PocketOS 0.1

1. Upload the three files (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest) together to an HTTPS static hosting service such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.
2. Open the hosted site in Safari on iPhone 12 mini while online.
3. Safari Share -> Add to Home Screen -> Add.
4. Launch from the new icon while online once, then test in airplane mode.
5. Turn iPhone landscape. If orientation is locked, unlock it in Control Center.

Limitations: iOS may still control app orientation; this cannot replace the iOS launcher. Terminal is a demo, not a real shell. Notes are local. iOS may evict web app cache under storage pressure. Do not rely on this for critical offline files.
